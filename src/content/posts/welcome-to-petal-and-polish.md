Hi, I'm glad you're here.

I'm **[Your Name Here]**, and Petal & Polish is the little corner of the internet where I'm documenting my (very real, very ongoing) journey into nail art and nail care. I'm not a licensed nail tech, and I'm not pretending to be one — I'm someone who got obsessed with a $12 set of dotting tools during a slow weekend and never looked back.

## Why this blog exists

I started painting my own nails a couple of years ago mostly out of boredom, and somewhere along the way it turned into a genuine hobby. Then it turned into a *problem* — I now own more nail polish than I probably should admit to, a drawer full of brushes I use maybe twice a month, and strong opinions about cuticle oil.

What I didn't expect was how much I'd end up learning about nail health along the way. Chasing the "perfect manicure" means paying attention to what's actually happening underneath the polish — ridges, peeling, brittleness, all of it. That side of things turned out to be just as interesting as the art, so this blog covers both.

## What you'll find here

A few things, roughly:

- **Nail art** — techniques I'm learning, trends I'm trying, and the occasional "here's what happened when I attempted a design way above my skill level."
- **Nail health basics** — plain-language notes on nail anatomy, common issues, and what's generally worth keeping an eye on. I'll say this clearly now and keep saying it: I'm not a doctor, and nothing here is meant to replace an actual professional. Think of it as the kind of thing a friend who's done a lot of reading would tell you over coffee, not a diagnosis.
- **General musings** — because sometimes I just want to talk about a product I loved, a salon visit that went sideways, or a lesson learned the hard way (RIP to the towel I ruined with acetone).

## A quick note on expectations

I'm learning in public here. Some of my early nail art attempts are going to be a little wobbly, and that's the point — I'd rather show the real process than a highlight reel that makes it look effortless. If you're also just starting out, I hope this ends up being useful, or at least good company.

Thanks for stopping by. Let's see where this goes.
